# Glossary

## What Is This?

A simple dictionary of terms used throughout the quick commerce platform documentation. If you encounter an unfamiliar word, look it up here.

---

## A

**Admin**
The person or team that runs and manages the entire platform — approving sellers and riders, managing orders, handling money, and configuring settings.

**Available balance**
Money in a seller's or rider's wallet that can be withdrawn right now. Different from pending balance.

---

## C

**Cash in hand**
The amount of physical cash a delivery partner is currently holding from cash-on-delivery orders. Must be submitted to the platform.

**Cash on delivery (COD)**
A payment method where the customer pays the delivery partner in physical cash at the doorstep instead of paying online at checkout.

**Category**
A way to organize products — like "Dairy", "Snacks", or "Beverages". The platform uses a three-level category system.

**Checkout**
The process where a customer reviews their cart, selects address and payment method, and places the order.

**Checkout group**
When a customer buys from multiple shops in one checkout, the system creates a grouped checkout experience with separate orders per shop.

**Commission**
The percentage of each sale that the platform takes from the seller's earnings.

**Customer**
A person who shops on the platform — browses products, places orders, and receives deliveries.

---

## D

**Dark store**
A warehouse or shop that stocks products specifically for quick commerce delivery — not a regular walk-in store.

**Delivery code**
A short code shown to the customer when the rider arrives. The rider must enter this code to confirm delivery. Also called OTP (one-time password).

**Delivery fee**
The charge for delivering an order, usually based on distance from shop to customer.

**Delivery partner**
A person (rider) who picks up orders from shops and delivers them to customers. Also called a rider or delivery boy.

**Delivery radius**
The maximum distance from a shop within which customers can place orders.

---

## E

**Experience section**
A curated row of products on the home page, configured by admin — like "Breakfast Essentials" or "Top Picks".

---

## F

**Fleet**
All delivery partners currently online and available (or actively delivering) on the platform.

**Free delivery threshold**
The minimum order value above which delivery is free (e.g., free delivery on orders above ₹500).

---

## G

**Grand total**
The final amount a customer pays — including items, delivery fee, platform fee, tax, tip, minus discounts and wallet deductions.

**GST**
Goods and Services Tax — a government tax applied to orders as configured by admin.

**Guest cart**
A shopping cart saved on the customer's device before they log in. Merges with their account cart when they log in.

---

## H

**Hyperlocal**
Serving a very small geographic area — typically a few kilometers — with fast delivery from nearby shops.

---

## L

**Ledger**
A complete record of every money movement on the platform — every credit, debit, payment, refund, and payout.

**Live tracking**
Showing the delivery partner's real-time position on a map as they deliver the order.

---

## M

**MRP**
Maximum Retail Price — the original printed price of a product. Shown alongside the selling price to display discounts.

**Multi-shop checkout**
When a customer buys items from two or more different shops in a single checkout experience.

---

## O

**Offer**
A promotional deal — shown as banners or grouped sections on the home page and offers page.

**Online payment**
Paying through a payment partner using UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, or digital wallet.

**Order lifecycle**
The complete journey of an order from placement to delivery (and possible return).

**OTP (One-Time Password)**
A short code used for login verification or delivery confirmation. Sent via text message or shown in the app.

**Out for delivery**
The stage when the delivery partner has picked up the order and is heading to the customer's address.

---

## P

**Pending balance**
Money earned by a seller that is on hold until the return window closes. Cannot be withdrawn yet.

**Pending approval**
The status of a seller or delivery partner application waiting for admin to approve or reject.

**Platform fee**
A service charge added to each order by the platform.

**Push notification**
An alert that appears on the user's phone even when the app is closed.

---

## Q

**Quick commerce**
A business model where everyday items are ordered online and delivered very fast (minutes to an hour) from nearby shops.

---

## R

**Return window**
The time period after delivery during which a customer can request a return (e.g., 30 minutes to a few hours).

**Rider**
See delivery partner.

---

## S

**Seller**
A shop or store owner who lists products on the platform and fulfills customer orders.

**Settlement**
The process of distributing money after a successful delivery — paying the seller, rider, and platform their shares.

**Shop by store**
A feature that lets customers browse products organized by seller/shop.

**Stock**
The number of units of a product available for sale. Reduced when orders are placed, restored when orders are cancelled.

**Support ticket**
A help conversation between a customer and the support team about a problem or question.

---

## T

**Text message code**
A short numeric code sent to the user's phone for login verification. See OTP.

**Tip**
An optional extra amount the customer can add at checkout that goes to the delivery partner.

---

## U

**UPI**
Unified Payments Interface — a popular instant payment method in India, used for online payments.

---

## W

**Wallet**
A digital money account within the platform. Customers use it to pay for orders. Sellers and riders use it to track earnings and request withdrawals.

**Withdrawal**
Transferring earned money from the platform wallet to a bank account. Requires admin processing.

**Workflow status**
The detailed internal stage of an order — more specific than the simple status shown to customers (e.g., "delivery search" vs "confirmed").

**Wishlist**
A saved list of products the customer is interested in but not ready to buy yet.
