# Delivery Partner Earnings and Cash

## What Is This?

Delivery partners earn money for every completed delivery. They also handle cash collected from customers on cash-on-delivery orders. This document explains earnings, COD cash management, and how to withdraw money to a bank account.

---

## Who Uses This?

**Approved delivery partners** tracking income and managing cash collections.

---

## What Can You Do?

- View per-delivery and total earnings
- See daily and weekly earnings summaries
- View order delivery history
- Track cash collected from COD orders
- Submit collected cash to the platform
- Request bank withdrawals

---

## How Riders Earn Money

### Per delivery

When a rider completes a delivery:

1. System credits the delivery fee to rider's wallet
2. If customer added a tip at checkout, tip is included
3. Earnings appear in rider's wallet immediately

### Earnings components

| Component    | Description                                |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Delivery fee | Base payment for the trip                  |
| Tip          | Optional amount customer added at checkout |
| Bonus        | Platform incentives (if configured)        |

### Example

- Delivery fee: ₹40
- Customer tip: ₹10
- Rider earns: ₹50 for this delivery

## COD Cash Handling

### What is COD cash?

When a customer chooses Cash on Delivery, the rider collects physical cash at the doorstep. This cash belongs to the platform (it includes product payment, fees, etc.) and the rider must eventually hand it over.

### Cash in hand

The rider's app tracks how much COD cash they are currently holding. This is called **cash in hand**.

### How cash accumulates

1. Rider delivers a COD order
2. Rider collects cash from customer
3. Rider confirms cash collected in the app
4. Cash in hand amount increases

### Submitting cash

Riders must regularly submit collected cash to the platform:

1. Rider goes to COD Cash page
2. Rider sees total cash in hand
3. Rider submits/pays the cash (through app or physically to admin)
4. Cash in hand amount decreases
5. Transaction recorded in history

### Why this matters

- Platform needs to reconcile COD money with actual orders
- Rider cannot keep customer cash indefinitely
- Admin tracks which riders owe how much cash

### Balance types

- **Earnings balance** — delivery fees and tips earned (can be withdrawn)
- **Cash in hand** — COD cash collected (must be submitted, not withdrawn)

### Transaction history

All money movements:

- Delivery earnings credited
- Tips received
- Cash collected (COD)
- Cash submitted to platform
- Withdrawals processed

---

## Withdrawals

### How to withdraw earnings

1. Rider goes to Withdrawals (in profile section)
2. Rider enters amount (up to available earnings balance)
3. Rider submits request
4. Admin processes the bank transfer
5. Status updates: pending → processed

### Rules

- Can only withdraw **earnings** — not cash in hand
- Cash in hand must be submitted separately
- Bank details must be set up in profile
- Minimum withdrawal amount may apply
- Processing takes 1–3 business days

---

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

- Delivery earnings credited automatically at delivery confirmation
- COD cash tracked separately from earnings
- Admin can see each rider's cash in hand on the cash collection dashboard
- When rider submits cash, admin records the settlement
- Withdrawal requests queued for admin processing
- All transactions permanently recorded

---

## Important Rules

- Always collect the exact COD amount shown on screen
- Confirm cash collection in the app immediately after collecting
- Submit cash regularly — do not accumulate large amounts
- Earnings and COD cash are separate — do not mix them up
- Withdrawals only from earnings, never from cash in hand
- Tips are yours to keep (included in earnings)

---

## What If Something Goes Wrong?

| Problem                        | What happens                           |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Earnings not showing           | Wait until delivery is fully confirmed |
| Cash in hand seems wrong       | Check COD history; contact admin       |
| Customer paid wrong COD amount | Contact support before confirming      |
| Withdrawal delayed             | Admin processing may take a few days   |
| Cannot submit cash             | Contact admin for manual settlement    |
