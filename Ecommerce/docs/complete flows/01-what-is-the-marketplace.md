# What Is This Marketplace?

## What Is This?

Arise is a **Flipkart-type multi-vendor ecommerce marketplace**. Customers browse a national catalog of products from many sellers, pay online or with cash on delivery where eligible, and receive orders through **standard shipping**. Last-mile delivery will use **third-party courier partners** later — not an owned rider fleet and not minutes-scale delivery.

This platform connects customers, sellers, and platform operators. Courier companies are fulfilment partners, not a first-party “delivery app” in v1.

---

## How Is This Different from Minutes Delivery?

| This marketplace                         | Minutes-delivery platforms                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Delivery in days via courier             | Delivery in minutes to an hour                     |
| Sellers and warehouses across regions    | Nearby local shops or dark stores                  |
| Planned, mixed-catalog orders            | Small, urgent, everyday orders                     |
| Third-party courier tracking             | Dedicated two-wheeler riders on the platform       |
| Wide catalog (fashion, electronics, …)   | Focus on essentials near the customer              |

The system is built around **catalog, checkout, seller fulfilment, and courier shipping** — not speed and proximity as the core product.

---

## Who Is Involved?

### 1. Customers

People who shop on the customer app: browse products, place orders, pay, and track shipments.

### 2. Sellers

Merchants who list products, hold stock, pack orders, and hand shipments to a courier when shipping is enabled.

### 3. Third-party courier partners (later)

External logistics companies that pick up packed orders and deliver them to the customer. Arise does not operate its own rider fleet in this model.

### 4. Platform admin

The team that runs the marketplace — approving sellers, managing the catalog, handling money, resolving problems, and configuring the platform.

---

## How Does the Platform Make Money?

The platform typically earns revenue through:

- **Delivery / shipping fees** — charged to the customer when applicable
- **Platform fees** — a service charge on each order
- **Commission** — a percentage taken from seller earnings
- **Other charges** — such as packaging or convenience fees, depending on configuration

Sellers earn from product sales (minus platform commission). Courier partners are paid through their own contracts when last-mile is integrated.

---

## A Typical Order Journey

1. **Customer** opens the app, browses the catalog, and adds items to the cart.
2. **Customer** checks out, chooses a delivery address, applies a coupon if they have one, and pays online or chooses cash on delivery where eligible.
3. **System** creates the order and notifies the **seller**.
4. **Seller** confirms, packs, and generates shipment details.
5. When courier integration is live, a **third-party courier** picks up the parcel and moves it through Shipped → In transit → Out for delivery → Delivered.
6. **Customer** tracks the shipment in the app using courier-provided events.
7. **System** settles money — seller earnings (held briefly for returns) and the platform’s share.

Delivery takes **days**, not minutes.

---

## What Makes This Platform Complete?

- **Customer app** for shopping, payments, orders, returns, wallet, and support
- **Seller portal** for catalog, inventory, and fulfilment
- **Admin console** for operations, catalog, users, and finance
- **Online payments** and **cash on delivery**
- **Customer wallet** as store credit (refunds, gift cards, referrals)
- **Return and refund** handling, including courier pickup
- **Support tickets** and help content
- **Marketing tools** — coupons, offers, banners, home page sections

A first-party rider app, live bike tracking, and minutes SLAs are **out of scope**.
