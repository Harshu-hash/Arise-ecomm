# Support Tickets and Reviews

## What Is This?

Customers sometimes need help, and their opinions matter. This document covers the support ticket system (help chat) and the product review system — how customers use them and how admins manage them.

---

## Who Uses This?

- **Customers** creating support tickets and writing reviews
- **Admins** responding to support and moderating reviews

---

## Support Tickets

### What is a support ticket?

A conversation between a customer and the support team about a problem or question. It works like a chat — messages go back and forth until the issue is resolved.

### How customers create a ticket

1. Customer goes to Support page in the app
2. Customer selects a topic or describes their issue:
   - Order not delivered
   - Wrong items received
   - Payment problem
   - Refund not received
   - Account issue
   - Other
3. Customer writes their message
4. Ticket is created and sent to admin support inbox

### How the conversation works

1. Customer sends a message
2. Admin sees the ticket in their support inbox (with unread badge)
3. Admin reads the issue and replies
4. Customer sees the reply instantly (real-time)
5. Customer can reply back
6. Conversation continues until resolved
7. Admin marks ticket as resolved

### Real-time updates

Both customer and admin see new messages instantly without refreshing — like a messaging app. This makes support feel fast and responsive.

### Multiple tickets

Customers can have multiple open tickets for different issues. Each ticket is a separate conversation.

---

## Admin Support Inbox

### What admin sees

- List of all support tickets
- Unread count badge for new messages
- Filter by status: open, resolved
- Customer name and issue topic

### Best practices for admin

- Respond promptly — customers expect quick help
- Be professional and empathetic
- Reference order numbers when relevant
- Mark resolved only when the issue is actually fixed
- Escalate complex issues (payment disputes, missing orders)

---

## Product Reviews

### How customers leave reviews

1. Customer receives a delivered order
2. Customer opens the product page or order detail
3. Customer gives a star rating (1 to 5 stars)
4. Customer writes a text review (optional)
5. Review is submitted

### Review moderation

Not all reviews appear immediately. Admin moderates them first:

1. Review goes into a "pending" queue
2. Admin reads the review
3. Admin **approves** (appears on product page) or **rejects** (hidden)
4. Rejection reasons: spam, inappropriate language, fake review, not about the product

### Why moderation matters

- Prevents fake or competitor reviews
- Keeps product pages trustworthy
- Removes offensive content
- Ensures reviews are from actual buyers

### What customers see

On product pages, approved reviews show:

- Star rating
- Review text
- Reviewer name (or anonymous)
- Date

## FAQ System

### What are FAQs?

Frequently Asked Questions — a list of common questions and answers displayed in the customer app's help section.

### Admin manages FAQs

- Add new questions and answers
- Edit existing FAQs
- Delete outdated FAQs
- Set display order

### Examples

- "How do I track my order?"
- "What is your return policy?"
- "How do I use a coupon?"
- "What payment methods do you accept?"

FAQs reduce support ticket volume by answering common questions upfront.

---

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

- Support messages are delivered in real time to both parties
- Tickets are stored permanently for reference
- Reviews are queued for moderation before going public
- FAQ changes appear immediately in the customer app

---

## Important Rules

- Support tickets are private — only the customer and admin can see them
- Reviews should be from verified buyers (customers who purchased the product)
- Admin should moderate reviews regularly to keep product pages fresh
- Resolved tickets can be reopened if the customer replies again
- Inappropriate support language should be handled professionally

---

## What If Something Goes Wrong?

| Problem                            | What to do                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Customer not getting support reply | Admin should check inbox; respond promptly |
| Review stuck in pending            | Admin needs to approve or reject           |
| Customer unhappy with resolution   | Reopen ticket; escalate if needed          |
| Fake review suspected              | Reject during moderation                   |
| FAQ answer outdated                | Admin updates the FAQ                      |
